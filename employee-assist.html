Employee Assist & Web Chat
Powered by SoundHound AI  ·  AI Tools for Municipal Staff & Residents Online
Employee Assist, powered by SoundHound AI, is an internal AI voice and chat assistant built for municipal staff. It gives frontline employees, 311 agents, clerks, and department teams instant access to the knowledge they need to serve residents confidently — without searching through shared drives, waiting on a colleague, or escalating unnecessarily.

  • Instant policy and process lookups during live calls
  • Consistent answers across all agents, every shift
  • Reduces hold time while staff search for answers
  • New staff onboard faster with AI-guided support
🏛️  Clerks & Administrative Staff
  • Quickly surface bylaws, procedures, and forms

Web Chat — AI on Your Municipality's Website
What it does
An AI chat widget embedded on your municipality's public website. Residents ask questions in plain language and get instant, accurate answers — 24/7, without calling or waiting. Common queries handled: hours, services, permits, programs, payments, and more.
Why Edgeworx manages it
Trained on your website content and municipal knowledge base. Edgeworx handles setup, content training, and ongoing updates — your team doesn't manage the AI. Live on your website within weeks. Fully aligned with resident information needs, not sales automation.
